CrateX website files
====================
Upload everything in this folder to the root of your site (public_html, www, or your host's upload area).
index.html is the whole page: styles, scripts and logo are inside it.

index.html            the page
favicon-16/32.png     browser tab icons
apple-touch-icon.png  phone home-screen icon
assets/               logo files (the page works without them, apart from the SVG tab icon)

Before going live
- The demo request form does not send anywhere yet. Connect it to a form service or your own backend.
- Feature and pricing text is draft copy. Check it against what CrateX does.
- Fonts load from Google Fonts, so the page needs internet access to show the right typefaces.
